Lost your dog? Here's what to do

Practical missing-dog guidance for owners across the UK

If your dog has gone missing, act quickly but try to stay organised. Share clear, accurate information and contact the local services that can help. ALFIK can help you publish a missing-dog report and make the details available to the wider community.

What to do when your dog goes missing

  1. Check nearby safe places. Ask household members and neighbours to check gardens, sheds, garages and other places where your dog could be sheltered. Take care near roads and do not put yourself in danger.
  2. Contact your dog's microchip database. Report your dog missing and check that your contact details are current.
  3. Contact your local council or dog warden. Tell them where and when your dog was last seen and provide a description.
  4. Call nearby veterinary practices and animal rescue organisations. Give them your dog's description, photograph and a way to contact you.
  5. Share a clear missing-dog report. Include a recent photograph, colour, breed or type, distinctive markings, collar or harness, last-seen area and date.
  6. Tell the police if you suspect your dog has been stolen. Keep any relevant details and reference numbers.
  7. Update everyone when your dog is found. Close or update online reports and let the microchip database and local services know.
Safety tip: If your dog is frightened or loose near traffic, avoid chasing them into danger. Share sightings with the appropriate local services and follow their advice.

Report your missing dog with ALFIK

ALFIK is a UK-wide lost-and-found animal platform. You can create a report with your dog's photograph, description, last-seen location and date, then search community reports for possible matches. Suggested matches need to be checked carefully and are not confirmed identifications.
