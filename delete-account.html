ALFIK Account Deletion

Animals Lost and Found Information Keeper

You can request deletion of your ALFIK account and the personal information associated with it. You do not need to give a reason.

How to request deletion

  1. Visit the official ALFIK website.
  2. Use the website's contact options to send a request headed ALFIK account deletion request.
  3. Include the email address used for your ALFIK account so we can locate it. Do not send your password.
  4. We may ask you to verify that you own the account before carrying out the request.

Go to ALFIK contact options

What will be deleted

Once your request has been verified, we will delete your ALFIK account and the personal data associated with it, where we control that data. This includes your account profile and identifiers, saved pet profiles, animal reports linked to your account, uploaded photographs, private messages, in-app notifications, and pet care or medication information associated with your account.

Information that may be retained

Some limited information may need to be retained where required by law, to resolve a dispute, protect users and animals, investigate misuse, or maintain essential security and financial records. Where information must be retained, we will restrict its use to that purpose and keep it only for as long as necessary. Information held by independent service providers, such as payment providers, is also subject to their own legal retention duties and privacy policies.

Timing

We will review verified requests and aim to complete account and associated data deletion within 30 days. Copies held in routine backup systems may remain until those backups are overwritten, for up to 90 days after deletion from active systems. Any information that must be retained for a legal or security reason will be kept only for the applicable required period.

Deleting some information without closing your account

If you only want a particular pet profile, report, photograph or other item removed, contact us through the official website and describe what you want deleted. You can request this without asking us to close your account. We will review the request and explain if any part cannot be removed.

More information
