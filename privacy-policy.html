ALFIK Privacy Policy

Animals Lost and Found Information Keeper

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how ALFIK handles personal information when you use the ALFIK website and app, including animal reports, pet profiles, messaging, notifications, rescue tools and optional veterinary microchip lookup features.

Important: Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, or information that could put you or another person at risk in animal reports, messages or support requests.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

ALFIK is operated by Animals Lost and Found in Kent Ltd, a volunteer-led animal reunification and welfare initiative. For privacy questions or requests about your personal information, use the contact details published on the official ALFIK website: animalslostandfoundinkent.co.uk. If you contact us, include enough information for us to understand your request, but do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account information: email address, account identifier and account settings needed to create and manage your account.
  • Pet and animal information: pet names, species, descriptions, photographs, microchip details you choose to save, and relevant care or medication details you enter.
  • Lost and found reports: animal details, dates, descriptions, report status and the location information you provide.
  • Messages and notifications: messages you send through ALFIK and in-app alerts relating to your account, reports, matches or features you use.
  • Rescue, veterinary and Lifeline information: organisation details, verification information, and any help request or referral information you choose to submit.
  • Technical and security information: information needed to operate the service, protect accounts, prevent abuse and troubleshoot errors. Third-party security or hosting services may process technical information such as network identifiers as part of their operation.
  • Payment-related information: if you choose a paid membership, payment processing is handled through the payment provider. ALFIK should not receive or store your full payment-card details.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate ALFIK, create and manage accounts, display animal reports, maintain pet profiles, suggest possible matches, deliver in-app notifications, support private messaging, administer memberships, verify participating organisations, protect the service and respond to privacy or support requests.

Possible matches are suggestions, not confirmation that two reports concern the same animal. ALFIK cannot guarantee that an animal will be found or that a match is correct.

4. Reports and information visible to others

Information submitted to help reunite an animal may be shown to other ALFIK users or participating organisations where needed for the feature to work. Do not put your home address, private telephone number, confidential location or other personal information in a public-facing report unless you are comfortable sharing it. Use the privacy controls provided and check each form before submitting.

Private messages are intended for the relevant conversation participants. We may process message information to provide the messaging service, handle reports of abuse, enforce our rules and protect users.

5. Optional ChipConnect veterinary lookup

Pet owners can choose whether to allow verified ALFIK veterinary practices to check a saved microchip number. This option is off by default. If enabled, a verified participating practice may submit a microchip number for an exact lookup. If an opted-in pet matches, ALFIK is designed to notify the owner in-app and identify the practice and its published contact details.

The lookup is intended to avoid showing the owner's private contact details or the full microchip number to the practice. ALFIK may keep a security audit record of lookups without recording the full microchip number. Only enable this feature if you are comfortable with this use of your pet's information. Feature availability and operation may depend on account and verification requirements.

6. Legal grounds for processing

Where UK data protection law applies, we process personal information where it is necessary to provide a service you request, where we have a legitimate interest in running and securing ALFIK and supporting animal reunification, where you have given consent for an optional feature, or where we must meet a legal obligation. You can withdraw consent for an optional feature by turning it off; this does not affect processing that already took place lawfully.

7. Service providers and sharing

ALFIK relies on service providers to run the platform. Depending on the feature, these may include Supabase for authentication, database and storage services; Cloudflare for security checks; PayPal for membership payments; and map or geocoding providers for location-related features. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services under their own terms and privacy information.

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information where necessary to provide a feature you request, protect people or animals, address misuse, or comply with law. We do not promise that every third-party provider or recipient will be located in the UK; international processing may occur subject to applicable safeguards.

8. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, maintain account and animal-report history, provide requested features, resolve disputes, protect security and meet legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by information type and feature. Some technical or audit records may be retained for security and accountability. We aim to delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

9. Keeping information secure

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including account authentication, access controls and security monitoring. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Choose a strong, unique password and tell us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

10. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, and a portable copy of information you provided. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

To make a request, use the contact details published on the official ALFIK website. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

11. Children

ALFIK is not designed to invite children to provide personal information independently. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us through the official website so we can review the situation.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ALFIK's features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates when this version was published. Please review this page periodically.
